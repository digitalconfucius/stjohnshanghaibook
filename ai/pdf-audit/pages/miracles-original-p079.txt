Epilogue

Why do many people—including bishops,
clergy, -and laity—fail to recognize Vladika
John as "Blessed," let alone as a Saint, and
continue to hold back his glorification?

Are we so blind with our own cares and
ambitions—this life, materialism and modern-
ism—that, even though we call ourselves Or-
thodox Christians, say our prayers and keep
the traditions of the Holy Orthodox Church,
we somehow fail to understand what Ortho-
doxy really is, what our goal in this life
should be, and fail to use what little time we
have wisely?
