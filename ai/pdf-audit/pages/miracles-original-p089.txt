Of course, I have no adequate words to
express my gratitude to and love for the un-
forgettable archpastor. During the earthly
life of a bishop we liturgically exclaim:
"Through the holy prayers of our master, O
lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy
on us." But since "God is not the God of the
dead, but of the living" (Matt. 22:32),
according to the truthful words of our
Saviour Himself, I even today continue to calh
for the help of Saint John with that same
prayer.

Constantly thanking God for vouchsaf-
ing me to be a witness of His greatest saint,
through whose prayers I have not sunken en-
tirely into the vanity of this world, I have
no doubt that the day will come when the
earthly Church will also canonize Archbishop
John as one of those "of whom the world was
not worthy" (Heb. 11:38)—and we shall thank
God, wondrous in His saints.

Fr. George Larin
