With joy I want to share with you and
tell everyone about Archbishop John. I knew
him from Shanghai, where as a young girl I
attended commercial school. The bishop would
often visit our school and bless us children.
My whole life has been spent as it were
under his wonderworking protection; other-
wise, it is impossible to explain the many
cases when my life was’ miraculously
preserved from unavoidable death through his
holy prayers which protected me.
