40.
A Paschal Resurrection
from Near Death

A nurse from the Jewish hospital in
Shanghai told the following:

During Pascha week, Blessed John came
to the hospital in order to visit some Russian
Orthodox people who were there. Going
through one of the wards, he stopped in
front of a screen that concealed the bed
where an elderly Jewish lady was dying. Her
relatives in the vestibule of the same hospital
were already expecting her death. Blessed
John raised the cross over her screen and
loudly exclaimed, "Christ is Risen!" The sick
lady came to and asked for water; and Bles-
sed John, going to the nurse, said, "The
sick lady wants to drink." The hospital per-
sonnel were amazed at the change in this la-
dy, who had just been about to die. Soon she
got well and left the hospital.

Vsevolod Alexandrovich Reyer
Brazil, South America
September 27, 1966
