What did it mean? Vladika had called us
toward him as if with impatience, as if it
were a matter of life or death. But it was I
who had seen Vladika, and my husband was
driving the car. I had said nothing to him,
had not warned him—there had been no time.
What did all this mean? At this time my hus-
band saw that I wasn't sleeping and told me
of our narrow escape. I told him that I knew,
that I hadn't been sleeping. "Vladika John
woke me up; he warned me." And I told him
what I had seen. My husband replied, "But
why didn't Vladika warn me, why didn't I see
him, and why didn't you tell me anything?" I
reflected. True, I had said nothing to him.
But how could Vladika have appeared to my
husband—after all, he was driving the car.
