47.
Healing of a Hopelessly
Sick Doctor

In the Russian colony in Shanghai in
the 1940's, there was a well-known doctor,
Nicholas C.,* who became gravely ill. His ail-
ment was so serious that one concilium of
doctors followed another. The best specialists
came from afar for consultations, but there
was no turn for the better in the condition
of the sick man, and his wife was made to
understand that she must be prepared for his
end.

During one difficult night of keeping
watch at the bed of her sick husband, Mrs.
C. could no longer endure the frightful des-
pair and hopelessness. Entrusting the watch
of the sick man to a nurse on duty, she ran
