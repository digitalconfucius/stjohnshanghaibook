Then came the morning of the day of
the commemoration of Vladika John's death.
In the Sepulchre under the cathedral there
was already much movement, the Pontifical
Divine Liturgy had begun. At this very time
"the Archbishop's Nina" breathed her last in
the hospital, and when her death was re-
ported in the Sepulchre, the memorial service
was celebrated right after the Liturgy not
only for Archbishop John, but also for her.
And so it turned out that the first memorial
service for her was an "Archbishop's" ser-
vice. Truly Vladika John took her to himself,
and from that time on she has been commemo-
rated in the Sepulchre together with Arch-
bishop John every year on the anniversary of

her death. Reported by T. Blinova
