I remember still another remarkable oc-
currence, or rather an incident in the life of
Blessed John during which I was personally
present. It was a usual weekday and the Li-
turgy was being celebrated by one of the
Shanghai cathedral priests. Blessed John was
standing on his usual spot, and I was appar-
ently serving in the altar—I don't remember
for sure. But I remember well how that priest
during his sermon was scolding the bishop,
pointing his finger at him and resorting to
such words as these: "a snake, a scorpion, a
toad, a hypocrite," etc. The bishop continued
to stand in his place, showing no reaction to
