25.
Help for a Sufferer of Rheumatism

Up to now I have not written about the
diseases of myself and my relatives of which
holy Vladika John healed us. I will do so
now. I can affirm under oath the exactness
and truth of my words.

In 1953 I began to be tormented by
rheumatism. On October 9 (of that year) in
Holy Trinity Monastery (Jordanville, N.Y.)
there was the opening of the Sobor of Bish-
ops, which Vladika John attended.

My sister Sophia went up to Vladika
and asked him to pray for the sick Olga (my-
self) and Lydia. Lydia is our niece who lives
in Australia. She had become ill and had been
placed in a hospital. On the very first night
(after this) my pains began to decrease, and
in about three days I was completely well and
did not have to begin taking the pills which
had been prescribed for me by the doctor,
and which I had bought for nine dollars.
