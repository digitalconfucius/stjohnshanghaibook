IV. Rescue From an Airplane Fire

In 1966 we went on vacation to Lake
Tahoe for a few days with our dear friend
Nina Holte and her husband. Returning home,
we took an airplane, while the Holte family
remained for a few more days in Tahoe. They
drove us to the airport and stood at the
landing strip to watch our plane take off. As
soon as I sat down in the airplane, I said
that nothing bad would happen to us because
Blessed John was with us. I always carry
with me a picture of Blessed John: no matter
where I travel, this photo of him is always
with me.
