to a conclusion: that the Lord is guiding all

III. Healing of a Gall Bladder Disorder

In 1955 we moved from Brazil to the
United States and settled in San Francisco,
California. Here the first of my gall bladder
attacks occurred, and I subsequently had a
total of nine such attacks. In 1961 was the
worst, ninth attack. I was placed in Moffat
Hospital on the lst of January, and on the
2nd I underwent surgery. As soon as. they
opened me up, the gall bladder broke, and
there was no hope of saving my life. But a
