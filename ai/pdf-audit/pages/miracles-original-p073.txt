I want to let you know of two small but
far from insignificant events which happened
to me last night.

disordered and on edge. I felt greatly frus-
trated in my inability to focus on any single
activity. I was particularly disturbed by the
fact that I had been unable to complete any
piece of writing I'd begun in over two years.

desk but still could not make a decision as to
