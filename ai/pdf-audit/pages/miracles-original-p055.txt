The author of this present note was at

how all this happened.

On Sunday, September 2, after the Lit-
urgy in the Orthodox Church of St. Vladimir
in Miami, the priest served a panikhida for
Archbishop John. When Russians had been
staying on the island of Tubabao (in the
Philippines thirty years ago), thanks to the
ceaseless prayers of Vladika John the ty-
phoons usual for this place did not occur.
This was even noted by the local inhabitants,
who often saw Vladika praying at night and
signing the camp with the sign of the Cross.
Once in the Philippines a powerful typhoon
was heading in the direction of the camp. By
the prayers of Vladika John, near the island
the typhoon changed direction and did not
hit the camp.
