Help for the Paralyzed

Dear Fathers,

Having read the "Chronicle of the Ven-
eration of Archbishop John of Shanghai," I
cannot be silent. My father, Herbert Klin-
gart, on November 2, 1977, suffered a bad
stroke and was paralyzed without conscious-
ness for five days. In December there was a
consultation of twelve physicians. They stated
and signed that he would not be able to
speak or to walk, and said, "Be prepared for
the worst."
