It was wonderful! —A miraculous healing for

I was afraid to tell anyone lest the
healing and freedom from pain would go away
and because it was a cherished and personal
experience. (In retrospect, I think also be-
cause I had tried to hide from everyone that
I was in so much pain—I had always taken
pride in the fact that I worked hard despite
the pain and covered it up.) But finally, I
had to tell Rev. S.—partially out of joy and
partially because I had used the oil without
his permission. That is when he told me to
write an account down and send it to The Or-
thodox Word.

In the couple of weeks that followed,
sometimes I would feel stiff or a twinge of
pain, but underneath it all was the strong
assurance and faith and knowledge that, no
matter the outside pain, the deep pain inside
had been healed through John Maximovitch's
help and prayers to God. I felt renewed in
body, soul, mind and spirit. Praised be our
Lord, and thanks to Blessed John!
