The bishop heard, with his boundlessly
loving paternal heart, the sorrowful plea of
his spiritual son, and, as he had done many
times before, hastened to help him, for he
had heard across the 7,000 miles which divid-

Archimandrite Mitrophan

was healed by the prayers of our beloved Vla-
